A few things before you begin.

Can SofaMate chat with me?

The current version offers companionship through video. AI chat, voice conversations and real-time interaction are not included.

Do I need an account?

Importing, playback, browsing and downloads do not require an account. Publishing and managing works does. Email codes, Google and GitHub use one shared Aveniqa account.

Are my videos uploaded automatically?

No. Videos and nine-frame previews stay on your computer unless you choose to share. Only share content you have the right to distribute.

Is it resource-heavy?

The download is about 1.95 MiB and uses system WebView2 without bundling a browser. Stop destroys the desktop player while keeping the tray. Playback resource use depends on video resolution and encoding.

Can each monitor have a different wallpaper?

Yes. Choose a wallpaper, quality and fit mode for each monitor. The default fills the screen by cropping without distortion. You can also keep the full image with borders.

How do official scenes play?

Evening living room contains 28 clips. Playback starts randomly and chooses among the defined next clips, with smooth transitions. Imported single videos use ordinary looping.
